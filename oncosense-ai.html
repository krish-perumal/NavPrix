Overview

A research initiative in early cancer detection.

OncoSense AI is a long-term research initiative by NAVPRIX Global focused on advancing early cancer detection through artificial intelligence. The platform explores how AI can assist researchers by analyzing multimodal healthcare data — supporting future research into earlier detection, improved clinical decision support, and precision medicine.
